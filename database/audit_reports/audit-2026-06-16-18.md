2. **[HIGH] Bulk-resolve or archive the 2026-04-19 invoice-extractor error backlog.** 481 unresolved operational errors are entirely from one failed batch run nearly 2 months ago. They are dragging C2 to 18.75 (from a potential 100). If these errors are confirmed stale/non-actionable, mark them resolved with a batch UPDATE (e.g., `UPDATE error_log SET resolved=1 WHERE agent_name='invoice-extractor' AND timestamp < '2026-04-20' AND resolved=0`).
